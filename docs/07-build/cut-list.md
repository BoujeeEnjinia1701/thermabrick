## C1. Heater wells

Four pieces of 3/4 in black steel pipe, **692 mm** each, from one 10 ft (3,048 mm) stick. Crimp the bottom 25 mm of each closed in a vise, leaving a finished well of 667 mm. The well bottom stands 20 mm above the inner drum floor and the top ends 25 mm above the lid.

## C2. U-tube

Two 1-1/4 in x 36 in black nipples (legs), two 1-1/4 in 90 degree elbows and one 1-1/4 in x 6 in nipple. No cutting.
Assembled, the leg centers are 206.4 mm apart and each leg top stands 152 mm above the jacket top. The bend centerline sits 50 mm above the inner floor.

## C5. Sand

68 kg of dry washed sand, filled to **491 mm above the inner floor** (42.5 L). Mark the level on the inside of the drum wall before filling. The headspace above it is 170 mm.

## C6. Base

Four K-23 bricks stood on edge (230 x 64 x 114), radial at 45, 135, 225 and 315 degrees, centered 150 mm from the drum axis (inner ends 35 mm, outer ends 265 mm). The drum chime bears across each brick. Pack stone wool between the bricks to the full base diameter of 879 mm.

## C9. Top insulation

Two discs of 89 mm batt, 345 mm diameter, over the lid, to 178 mm. Slit for the legs, heater leads and thermocouple probes.

## C10. Jacket

Top cap: a 910 mm disc from two overlapped strips, with a 15 mm turned-down edge, and holes for both legs (44.2 mm), the heater leads and the thermocouple leads. Flashing used: about 7.4 m of the 7.6 m roll.
